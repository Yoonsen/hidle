Høringssvar fra UBAGO GROUP

Dato:
02.01.2023

TO WHOM IT MAY CONCERN
UBAGO GROUP is a Spanish Seafood Business Group constituted by two companies established in the South of Spain
-
UBAGO GROUP MARE S.L. (Ctra, Higuerón, 135,11300, La Línea de la Concepción, Cádiz, SPAIN. VAT NUMBER: B29791258).
-
AHUMADOS UBAGO S.L. (
Calle.
Charles Darwin, 3, 29590 Málaga, SPAIN. VAT NUMBER: B92277078).
Specialized in fish and seafood processing in three main areas: Smoked Fish, Salted Fish and Canned Fish.
Within these three production divisions, UBAGO GROUP leads the Smoked Salmon Production in Spain with a sales quota (sum of both companies) that exceeds 50% in the National Retail Sector.
This high quota requires huge purchases of Norwegian Aquaculture Atlantic Salmon reaching figures up to 10.000 tonnes per year.
Being a Seafood Business Group in which the part of its Value Proposal is based on the purchase and transformation of Norwegian Aquaculture Salmon, the new implemented measure done by the Norwegian Government on the use of
aquaculture marine resource tax, has a huge negative impact on its business activity
.
Due to:
-
The increase in taxes applied to farmed salmon during its growth phase at sea cages (+ 40 %).
-
T
he fact that
tax rate is tied to the Nasdaq spot price, a price that only covers a minor share of Norwegian sales of Norwegian Superior Quality Salmon.
-
The great fluctuation of salmon spot price under this indicator (even weekly changes).
-
The existing uncertainty regarding its tax regulations (in valuation until June 2023).
Commercial relations between Norwegian Aquaculture producers (Farmers) and European companies that produce smoked salmon - such as the UBAGO GROUP - are being seriously damaged, causing a series of problems:
a)
The practical impossibility to establish annual contracts and purchase agreements that guarantee the Supply of Raw Material at established fixed prices as it has been done currently.
b)
An increase in salmon purchase prices (values that can reach even an increase over +50%).
c)
No visibility of salmon price trends through the year; the indicative tool used in the past to know price trends - and be able to establish purchase agreements - was the Fish Pool Price. Under this new situation, this indicator has no sense.
d)
Problems to fix price agreements with Retailers; the price variability suffered by salmon raw material cannot be transferred to the Retail sector used to establishing stable sales prices for long periods.
In addition to these serious negative inputs, the increasing prices for Norwegian Salmon is causing in the European Market a forced price increase for final consumers, which is leading to sales drops and Salmon Market instability.
Facing this new situation, UBAGO GROUP would like to express its deep concern about the new context originated by the implementation of this new tax on Norwegian aquaculture salmon and its and its form of application
Because of that, urgent actions are requested to Norwegian Government to return to normal business activities that guarantee stability in the supply chain and that would permit adequate exploitation and transformation of these seafood resources as well as its impact on the final consumer.
